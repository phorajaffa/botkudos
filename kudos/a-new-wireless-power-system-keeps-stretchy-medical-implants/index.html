A New Wireless Power System Keeps Stretchy Medical Implants Running in a Moving Body

South Korean researchers built a stretchable liquid-metal receiver that keeps implants powered even as the body twists and deforms. In animal tests it ran a soft cardiac pacemaker reliably, a step toward implants that conform to living tissue without wires.
