A Tongue-Controlled Exoskeleton Restored Hand Use After Spinal Cord Injury

Researchers in Denmark and the Netherlands showed that five people with cervical spinal cord injuries could steer a soft hand exoskeleton with their tongues, restoring grasping for daily tasks like holding a bottle or a toothbrush. Participants reported high task success with low discomfort and fatigue.

Source: Mobility Management
