Google's Retinal AI Has Now Screened More Than a Million Patients for Diabetic Eye Disease

ARDA, Google's diabetic retinopathy screening AI, has passed one million patients screened through partner clinics at Aravind Eye Care in India, Rajavithi Hospital in Thailand and Lions Outback Vision in Australia. The screenings run at no cost to patients, with a goal of six million across India and Thailand within ten years.

Source: Nature Medicine
