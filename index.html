Battery Waste Became Pouch Cells That Last Nearly Twice as Long

October 2026 · Science Kudos level: Bravo

Researchers at Worcester Polytechnic Institute turned mixed battery waste into upgraded cathodes and graphite anodes, then built full pouch cells from nothing but the upcycled materials. The cells held 70% of their capacity after 2,590 cycles, nearly double the life of cells made with commercial materials, in research published in Advanced Energy Materials.

The FDA Approved the First Heart Valve That Grows With Children

October 2026 · Medicine Kudos level: Legend

The FDA approved Edwards Lifesciences' Autus valve on October 1, the first heart valve designed to expand as a child grows, from 13 mm up to adult size via a balloon catheter. In a 62-patient trial every child was free of device complications at 30 days, and the polymer leaflets could spare kids from repeat open-heart surgeries.
